Privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Life in UK Prep is made by TAF TECH LABS SRL. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in it stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves a few things on your device only:

  • your answers, mistakes and progress
  • your test date, if you add one
  • your explanation language and reminder settings

We can't see any of this. Deleting the app deletes it.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone. They are created on the device and don't pass through any server of ours. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that purchase.

Reporting a mistake

"Report a mistake" opens an email in your own mail app, already filled in with the question number, the app version and the iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. If you do, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to look into the report and reply. We delete these emails once the report is dealt with, and at the latest after 12 months.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't identify you. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the Life in the UK test. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. If you've emailed us, you can ask us to delete those emails. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the text in the app.
